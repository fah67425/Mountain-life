#Mountain Life

**A mountain lifestyle simulator built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.**

Welcome to the Highlands. Life moves differently up here.

In Mountain Life, you control an explorer living in a peaceful mountain valley. Manage your character's needs, earn money, explore the surroundings, gain experience and progress through different levels.

## Play the Game

**Live Website:** https://fah67425.github.io/Mountain-life/

**Recommended:** Desktop or laptop for the best experience.

## Features

### 1. Character Profile
- Your own explorer character.
- Character level and title.
- Experience points (XP).
- XP progress bar.
- Level progression system.

### 2. Character Needs
Manage four important character statistics:

- Health: Your character's physical condition.
- Energy: Determines how much activity you can perform.
- Hunger: Decreases when performing certain activities.
- Happiness: Reflects your character's mood.

Each statistic has a visual progress bar.

### 3. Daily Activities

There are four activities available:

| Activity | Reward / Effect | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Work | +$30, -20 energy, -15 hunger, -5 happiness | 3 hours |
| Eat | -$10, +35 hunger, +5 happiness | 1 hour |
| Explore | +25 XP, -25 energy, -15 hunger, +20 happiness | 2 hours |
| Rest | +35 energy, +5 health | 2 hours |

Weather can change the effects of exploration.

### 4. Economy System
- Start with $100.
- Work to earn money.
- Spend money on food.
- Manage your available balance.

### 5. Level Progression
- Earn XP by exploring.
- Every 100 XP increases your level.
- Receive $50 whenever you level up.
- Unlock new character titles as you progress.
- Every 5 levels increases your maximum energy by 10%.

### 6. Level-Up Notifications
- Floating black popup.
- White text and level information.
- Displays your level-up rewards.
- Continue button to close the notification.

### 7. Dynamic Weather
Experience different weather conditions:

- Sunny: Exploration provides extra happiness.
- Cloudy: Normal weather conditions.
- Rainy: Exploration consumes additional energy.
- Stormy: Exploration consumes even more energy.

Weather can change when a new day begins.

### 8. Time and Day System
- In-game clock.
- Daily progression.
- Activities consume different amounts of time.
- A new day begins when the clock reaches midnight.

### 9. Activity Journal and Live Event Center
Keep track of your daily activities.

Features include:
- Automatic event notifications.
- Activity categories.
- Work, Food, Explore, Rest and System filters.
- Expandable event descriptions.
- Event timestamps and day information.
- Daily activity counter.
- Clear history button.

## How to Play

1. Open the game using the website link.
2. Start with $100 and your initial character statistics.
3. Choose an activity from the Daily Activities section.
4. Monitor your health, energy, hunger and happiness.
5. Work to earn money.
6. Eat to restore hunger.
7. Rest to recover energy.
8. Explore to earn XP.
9. Watch your character level up and collect rewards.
10. Check the weather before exploring.
11. Use the Activity Journal to review your progress.

## Tips for Players

- Keep your energy above the required amount before working or exploring.
- Eat regularly to maintain your hunger.
- Rest when your energy gets low.
- Explore to gain XP and unlock higher levels.
- Pay attention to weather conditions before exploring.
- Save your money by planning your activities.

## Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript

No external game engine is required.

## Compatibility

Mountain Life is a browser-based project.

Desktop and laptop browsers are recommended for the best experience. The interface also includes basic responsive styling for smaller screens.

## Current Limitations

- Game progress does not persist after refreshing the browser.
- Weather is randomized when a new day begins.
- The game currently features a limited selection of activities.
- Additional locations, equipment and upgrades can be introduced in future updates.
